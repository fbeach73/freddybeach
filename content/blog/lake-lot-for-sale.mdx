A pristine **1.32-acre lakefront property** on Lake George, New Brunswick is available for purchase. This large, undeveloped lot features **130 feet of private shoreline**, making it ideal for building a dream home, cottage, or summer getaway. The property offers year-round road and power access in a tranquil setting.

## Build Your Dream on the Water

The location provides opportunities to build a private dock and enjoy stunning sunsets surrounded by unspoiled nature. Lake George is known as the **cleanest spring-fed lake in New Brunswick**, with an average depth of 12-15 feet, perfect for canoeing, kayaking, paddle boarding, and swimming. The property is surrounded by a diverse mix of birch, maple, fir, and spruce trees.

## Community & Recreation

The Lake Association on Lake George serves as a valuable resource for residents. The area features maintained **ATV trails and snowmobile trails** managed by provincial associations in York West. Evening activities include experiencing the distinctive calls of loons, bullfrogs, and Canadian geese during spring and summer months.

## Location & Accessibility

Harvey Lake, situated in Harvey Station just **5 minutes away**, offers dining and the oldest county store in New Brunswick. The property is conveniently located **30 minutes from Fredericton**, the provincial capital. Currently, the lot features only a cleared pathway from the access road off Route 636 to the lakefront.
