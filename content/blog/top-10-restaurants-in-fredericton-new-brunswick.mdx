Fredericton's restaurant scene offers a mouthwatering journey through **maritime and Canadian cuisine**. You'll love Maxwell's Steak & Seafood Magic for **fresh local seafood**, while STMR. 36 BBQ delivers waterfront flavors with **innovative BBQ techniques**. St. James Gate serves up Canadian-inspired dishes on a golf course, and Isaac's Way celebrates community through **creative local ingredients**. For fine dining, Catch Urban Grill provides elegant global inspirations, and Wolastoq Wharf impresses with **sustainable seafood options**. Claudine's Eatery brings East Coast comfort food, The Palate offers fusion experiences, and Boyce Farmer's Market showcases the region's freshest produce. Your culinary adventure is just beginning.

-   Maxwell's Steak & Seafood Magic offers an elegant dining experience featuring local sustainable seafood and a romantic waterfront atmosphere in Fredericton.

## Maxwell's Steak & Seafood Magic

Maxwell's Steak & Seafood Magic is a **classic steakhouse** nestled in the historic Crowne Plaza, Fredericton—Lord Beaverbrook Hotel.

The restaurant offers an exceptional dining experience with a distinctive Maritime twist, featuring handpicked steaks from across Canada and a focus on **local, sustainable seafood** including succulent lobster, fresh salmon, and briny oysters.

Executive Chef Matt Mackenzie curates an **elegant menu** that showcases the region's finest ingredients, complemented by an **extensive wine list**, hand-crafted traditional cocktails, and local craft beers.

The restaurant's dining space includes a **cozy interior** and a **waterfront patio** available from late May, providing guests with a versatile and inviting atmosphere.

Considered the finest dining establishment in Fredericton, Maxwell's has earned acclaim for its **romantic ambiance**, warmest Maritime hospitality, and **outstanding service**.

The restaurant consistently receives high recommendations for its exceptional food and engaging dining experience.

**Address:** Crowne Plaza, Fredericton—Lord Beaverbrook Hotel

**Hours:** Tuesday to Saturday – 5:00 PM-10:00 PM

## St. James Gate Culinary Delight

![st james gate cuisine](/images/blog/imported/st_james_gate_cuisine.jpg)

St. James Gate Culinary Delight is a distinctive restaurant nestled on the picturesque West Hills **golf course** in Fredericton's North Side.

Specializing in **Canadian-inspired cuisine**, the establishment prides itself on utilizing **fresh, local, and seasonal ingredients** to craft memorable dining experiences.

Beyond its restaurant offerings, St. James Gate provides extensive **event and banquet facilities**, catering to **weddings, meetings, and special occasions** with full service and professional support.

The restaurant goes beyond traditional dining by offering **extensive event planning services**.

Clients can take advantage of their extensive facilities, which include tables, chairs, black linen, napkins, basic tea lights, cutlery, glassware, and dedicated service staff.

Their event packages are flexible, requiring a **non-refundable deposit** to secure dates and spaces, with additional details managed through a structured booking process.

An intriguing aspect of St. James Gate is its commitment to creating **dynamic dining experiences**, including plans for **live music events** and the ability to keep the bar open as long as the party continues.

The venue's location on a golf course adds a unique atmospheric charm to its dining and event offerings.

**Address:** 200 Summerhill Row, Fredericton, NB, E3G 0C4, Canada

**Hours:** Tuesday to Saturday: 11:30 AM – 9:00 PM, Sunday and Monday: Closed (winter season)

**Phone:** (506) 206-9609

## Wolastoq Wharf Seafood Experience

![seafood dining at wharf](/images/blog/imported/seafood_dining_at_wharf.jpg)

Wolastoq Wharf offers a tantalizing **seafood dining experience** on Fredericton's Northside, nestled in the heart of New Brunswick. This fine dining restaurant specializes in an impressive array of **maritime cuisine**, featuring **locally sourced seafood** like fresh oysters, succulent lobster, and perfectly prepared scallops.

Beyond seafood, the restaurant also presents a **diverse menu** including chicken, beef, and pork selections that cater to various culinary preferences.

The restaurant's menu is a celebration of **Atlantic Canadian flavors**, with standout dishes such as **seafood chowder**, beer battered halibut, lobster stuffed haddock, and innovative creations like seafood risotto and maple glazed salmon.

Guests can also enjoy special offerings like the **Wharf Platter**, lobster roll, and shrimp po' boy sandwich, ensuring a memorable dining experience that showcases the region's rich gastronomic heritage.

Owned and operated by St. Mary's First Nation, Wolastoq Wharf has garnered significant recognition, including top marks from Trip Advisor and Where to Eat in Canada.

The restaurant prides itself on using locally sourced ingredients and offers a variety of dietary options, including **gluten-free** and **vegan selections**, making it an **inclusive dining destination** for food enthusiasts.

**Address:** 527 Union Street, Fredericton, New Brunswick, Canada

**Hours:** Monday to Sunday: 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM

**Phone:** 506-449-0100

## Isaac's Way Creative Cuisine

## Gahan House Riverside Brew

![riverside brew at gahan](/images/blog/imported/riverside_brew_at_gahan.jpg)

Nestled in the heart of downtown Fredericton's Historic Garrison District, **Gahan House Riverside Brew** stands as a representation of **local brewing tradition**. Part of a six-location chain across Atlantic Canada, this restaurant and brewery offers a unique culinary experience featuring **handcrafted ales**, a delectable menu, and fresh **East Coast oysters**.

Named after John Gahan, a 19th-century merchant and importer from Prince Edward Island, the establishment embodies a rich **historical narrative**.

The brewery boasts an **in-house brew system** that guarantees patrons enjoy the freshest beer possible, complemented by a welcoming atmosphere characteristic of all Gahan House locations.

While the original Gahan House was located in Charlottetown, this Fredericton location provides a **modern interpretation** of the classic maritime pub experience, offering **catering and private dining** options for guests.

The restaurant's commitment to local brewing and culinary excellence is further highlighted by its leadership, with General Manager Jarrett Coffie overseeing operations.

Its strategic location and diverse offerings make it a standout destination for both locals and visitors seeking an **authentic maritime dining experience**.

**Address:** 426 Queen Street, Fredericton, New Brunswick, E3B 1B6

**Hours:** Sunday to Thursday: 11:30 AM – 10 PM, Friday to Saturday: 11:30 AM – 11 PM

**Phone:** 506-454-1929

## The Palate Restaurant Fusion

![culinary fusion dining experience](/images/blog/imported/culinary_fusion_dining_experience.jpg)

The **Palate Restaurant and Cafe** is a **locally owned** dining establishment nestled in the heart of Fredericton's historic Garrison district. Founded on June 1, 2001, by Ken Roberts and now operated by Bella Hospitality, the restaurant offers a **casual fine dining** experience with an **open kitchen** atmosphere.

Its menu showcases a **diverse culinary landscape**, featuring everything from classic breakfast items like omelets and French toast to sophisticated dishes such as Tequila and Lime Shrimp and Pork Rillette.

The restaurant prides itself on sourcing ingredients from **local farmers, fishermen, and foragers**, creating a menu that celebrates the region's culinary bounty. An open kitchen allows diners to watch chefs prepare their meals, while **local artwork** adorns the walls, reflecting the restaurant's commitment to showcasing local talent and flavors.

Guests can also enjoy a rotating selection of locally produced **craft beers and ciders**, adding to the restaurant's vibrant dining experience.

The Palate's unique character stems from its origins, inspired by West Coast hospitality and enhanced by **Chef Boris Müller**'s European culinary background.

The restaurant's name itself is a reflection of the artistry in its food, creating a dining experience that goes beyond mere sustenance to become a celebration of local ingredients and **culinary craftsmanship**.

**Address:** Garrison District, Fredericton, NB

**Phone:** (506) 450-7911

## Boyce Farmer's Market Freshness

![fresh produce at market](/images/blog/imported/fresh_produce_at_market.jpg)

Nestled in the heart of Fredericton, the **Boyce Farmer's Market** is a **culinary paradise** that showcases the vibrant food culture of New Brunswick. This bustling marketplace offers an incredible array of fresh, **locally sourced products**, from ultra-fresh produce and organic meats to artisan chocolates and specialty coffees.

Visitors can explore a diverse selection of foods, including **fresh-squeezed juices**, New Brunswick wines, **ethnic delicacies** like Chinese buns and Lebanese stuffed grape leaves, and an enticing **Food Alley** featuring everything from falafel sandwiches to buffalo burgers.

The market is a sensory experience that goes beyond just food, presenting a colorful tapestry of **local craftsmanship**. Shoppers can browse one-of-a-kind gifts including **handcrafted pottery**, unique jewelry, local paintings, textiles, and even lawn sculptures.

With over 200 indoor and outdoor suppliers from the Saint John River Heritage Corridor, the market represents a true celebration of local agricultural and culinary talent.

Recognized as one of Canada's top 10 community markets, the Boyce Farmer's Market is more than just a shopping destination—it's a cultural institution with a rich history dating back to 1951.

Its reputation as a **community gathering spot** has made it an integral part of **Fredericton's local food scene**, offering residents and visitors alike a genuine taste of New Brunswick's finest produce and artisan creations.

**Address:** 665 George Street, Fredericton, NB E3B 1K4

**Hours:** Saturdays from 7 a.m. – 1 p.m.
